I’m Josh, chef and founder of Epochurian. Raised on a melting pot of Cantonese, Shanghainese, Egyptian, Russian, Dutch, and British kitchens, I started Epochurian so private dining would feel like a genuine adventure—where every course brings surprise, theatre, and unforgettable flavour.

We research and cook multi-course historical feasts in your own home across London (or in our private N10 garden venue). Between courses, we share the hidden stories and forgotten techniques behind every dish—turning your dinner party into a night your guests will talk about for years.
