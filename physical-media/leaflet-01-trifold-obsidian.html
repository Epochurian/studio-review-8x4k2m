The Founder & Chef

A Culinary Voyage Through Time

I'm Josh, chef and founder of Epochurian. Raised on a melting-pot of Cantonese, Shanghainese, Egyptian, Russian, Dutch and British cooking, I started at 10 and never stopped.

"I wanted people to feel like they were on an adventure, where the only guarantee was being well fed."

Every dish is researched from historical manuscripts and brought to life live at your table.
