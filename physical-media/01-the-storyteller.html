On the quiet art of dining across centuries, in your own home.

Restaurants are built on turnaround times. Tables must be cleared in two hours, the room is loud enough to swallow conversation, and the evening ends the moment a card machine arrives at your elbow.

Epochurian began with a different premise: that the finest dining room in London is already yours—if only someone would bring a lost century to your table.

We research, source, and cook multi-course historical banquets inside private homes. From 12th-century Levantine spice routes at the Court of Saladin, to 15th-century Ming imperial courts, to the gilded tables of 19th-century Tsarist Russia, every dish is reconstructed from archival recipes and balanced for modern culinary excellence.

We arrive discreetly with heirloom ingredients, period tableware, and an unhurried pace. As each course is served, we share the stories, forgotten techniques, and histories behind what is on your plate—then step back and let the night belong to your guests.

For smaller gatherings, our private garden venue in North London (N10) also opens for a limited number of seasonal commissions each year.
